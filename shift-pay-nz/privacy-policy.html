Privacy Policy

ShiftPay NZ (Android app com.uv.shiftpay) · Last updated 17 November 2026

In short: ShiftPay NZ works completely offline. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no internet access, no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and we never see your data.

What the app stores

To work out your pay and manage your wallet, the app saves the information you type in, such as:

  • Jobs, pay rates, tax codes and the shifts or earnings you log
  • Business expenses for self-employed work
  • Wallet accounts, transactions, recurring payments and budgets
  • Optional profile details: name, email, phone, IRD number, work rights and a profile photo
  • App settings, including a PIN if you turn on app lock. The PIN itself is not saved, only a secure one-way hash of it.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app cannot connect to the internet (it does not request the Internet permission), so no information is sent to us or to anyone else. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for reminders you choose, such as backup reminders, recurring payments and weekly visa-hours warnings.
  • Run at startup: so scheduled reminders still work after your phone restarts.

When you pick a backup folder, a file to restore, or a profile photo, Android's own picker gives the app access to only the file or folder you choose.

Backups, exports and sharing

  • Backups, CSV exports and PDF payslips are created only when you ask for them, and saved where you choose.
  • If you share a file (for example by email or a cloud drive), it goes through the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.
  • If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include this app's data in your Google account backup. You control this in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete entries in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you saved yourself are not removed automatically.

Children

The app is meant for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Pay and tax estimates

Pay, tax and other figures are estimates to help you plan. They are not financial or tax advice. Check your payslip or with Inland Revenue (IRD) for official amounts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
